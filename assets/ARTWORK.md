# Banner artwork

The Chao illustration is existing official artwork from SEGA's legacy Sonic Channel character page. It was not generated with AI.

- [Official Chao character page](https://sonic.sega.jp/SonicChannelOld/chara/chao/index.html)
- [Original illustration](https://sonic.sega.jp/SonicChannelOld/chara/chao/image/chara_chao.gif)
- The illustration was converted from GIF to PNG and proportionally resized for the 1200 × 320 banner. The character drawing was not redrawn.
